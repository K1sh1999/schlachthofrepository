About us

Repository

This research brings together two types of material. Collected data comes from direct observation and engagement: photographs, audio recordings, interviews, videos, field notes, and news. Scraped data is extracted from digital sources and existing information systems. The material is presented without a predetermined hierarchy or narrative. It remains fragmentary and heterogeneous.

A search reorganizes these fragments into a temporary composition. Images, voices, documents, and observations appear together according to the entered terms. Each query produces a different configuration and, through it, a speculative understanding of the site. The interface does not provide a complete or definitive reading. It creates the conditions for examining connections between fragments.

Scope

Developed within the Studio Caruso at ETH Zürich, this research focuses on the Schlachthof Zürich as the subject of the semester.

Repository

This research brings together two types of material.

Collected

Collected data comes from direct observation and engagement: photographs, audio recordings, interviews, videos, field notes, and news.

PhotographsAudio recordingsInterviewsVideosField notesNews
Scraped

Scraped data is extracted from digital sources and existing information systems.

Digital sourcesInformation systems
Material
The material is presented without a predetermined hierarchy or narrative. It remains fragmentary and heterogeneous.
Search
A search reorganizes these fragments into a temporary composition. Images, voices, documents, and observations appear together according to the entered terms. Each query produces a different configuration and, through it, a speculative understanding of the site.
Interface
The interface does not provide a complete or definitive reading. It creates the conditions for examining connections between fragments.

Understanding is produced through their relations.
